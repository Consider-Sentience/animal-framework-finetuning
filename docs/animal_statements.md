# The animal test statements

The paper's persona test uses general ethics statements and has no animal content. We add 60 agree or disagree statements about animals, written in the same format as the paper's. Twenty are in a welfarist register, twenty are in a rights register, and twenty are common ground, where both frameworks give the same answer. The common-ground statements check for a model that agrees with everything. For each framework, 30 expected answers are Yes and 30 are No, matching the paper's half-reversed design. The statements and the answer each constitution implies are in `statements/animal_statements.csv`.

The statements were written for this study and reviewed by the author. No human raters were used. Two language models, given only the constitutions and not our labels, were asked whether each constitution implies each statement. Neither gave the opposite answer to any of the 120 labels (60 statements, two constitutions each). Both answered AMBIGUOUS for at least one constitution on 10 statements, so the remaining 50 are reported as a secondary analysis that the two models support. The full 60 are the main analysis.

The authors' Consequentialist and Deontological constitutions, the Virtuous constitution and the Subordinate constitution have no answer key on these statements, because the key is written for the Welfarist and Rights-based constitutions. For every model, the framework-neutral measure is the share of the 40 framework-specific statements answered in the rights-expected direction, reported for the welfarist-worded and rights-worded statements separately.
